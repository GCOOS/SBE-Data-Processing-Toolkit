Walton-Smith (SBE911Plus with deck unit)
========================================

Based on WS24258 CTD cast files.

Method for Conductivity alignment: visually checking T,C,density,descent rate plots with a range of alignment values.
Method for Oxygen signal alignment: visually checking Oxygen (ml/l) plots (incl. up- and downcast!) with a range of alignment values.
These is described in SeaBird manuals.


1) Primary Conductivity: 0.0 seconds.
(According to manuals, deck unit can do the alignment for the primary sensors. Based on the plots it seems to do that.)

2) Secondary Conductivity: 0.073 seconds.
(This is the default value, and seems to agree with the plots.)

3) Oxygen: 5.0 seconds.
